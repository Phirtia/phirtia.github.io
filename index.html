About

Background & Education

I am a Data Scientist who combines rigorous modelling, interdisciplinary methodologies, and deep contextual understanding of policy and social development. After 8 years in applied research, I have built machine learning models, geospatial analyses, production data pipelines, and evidence-based reports for governments, NGOs, and global research firms across Brazil, Latin America, and the UK.

My path, from ethnographic fieldwork in Brazilian favelas and refugee communities, through electoral survey coordination, to building production ML systems in London, shapes everything about how I approach data: with technical rigour and human proximity in equal measure.

Large-Scale ML & NLP Pipelines

Production end-to-end NLP classification pipeline built on web-scraped corpora using TensorFlow Transformers, Sentence Transformers, embeddings, and vectorisation. Implements confidence-gap soft rejection, pseudo-label pipelines, and batched BLAS-level matrix inference for performance.

Data engineering layer includes idempotent pipeline design with upsert and constraint enforcement, staging-to-production promotion, GitHub Actions orchestration, composite key multi-source joins, and .jsonl audit trails. LLM Ops layer covers REST integration, prompt versioning with agreement-rate tracking, few-shot boundary disambiguation, and retry with output schema enforcement. Applied to payroll prediction and job market benchmarking in the UK.

What Sets Me Apart

My Differential

I sit at the intersection of three worlds that rarely overlap: advanced engineering, rigorous social science, and deep fieldwork with vulnerable communities. That combination shapes everything I build.

🔬

Interdisciplinary Foundation

A rare mix of Social Science BSc, Data Science MBA, and MPA at LSE, I speak the language of statisticians, policymakers, and community workers equally. I don't just model reality; I understand the context behind the numbers.

🌍

Field Research & Human Proximity

Years of ethnographic fieldwork, home-based interviews, and direct work with refugees, trafficking victims, and women in violence contexts. I build models that respect the people behind the data, because I have sat with them.

⚙️

Senior Engineering Depth

End-to-end pipeline ownership: from idempotent ETL design and CI/CD orchestration to LLM inference systems with prompt versioning and confidence-gap rejection. I architect production-grade systems, not just notebooks.

📐

Evidence for Policy, Not Just Papers

Every model I've built has been used by a government department, an NGO, or an international body. My outputs drive decisions at scale, from São Paulo's municipal human rights system to UNODC reports and the UK labour market.

🤝

Global South Perspective

Working across Brazil, Latin America, and the UK, I bring lived understanding of inequality, informality, and institutional fragility to data problems that Western-trained analysts often miss. I design for contexts where data is scarce.

🚀

Builder & Founder Mindset

I founded D3S Consultoria from scratch, designing its analytics infrastructure, volunteer programme, and global network. I know what it takes to ship, sustain, and scale an organisation alongside a full-time research career.

Data Advocacy

D3S Consultoria

D3S Consultoria is a Brazilian nonprofit I founded that provides free data analytics consulting, training, and technical products to NGOs that lack data expertise. Since 2024 we have impacted 25 organisations and indirectly reached over 35,000 people in vulnerable situations.

The organisation runs a volunteer programme to increase women's presence in STEM. In early 2026 we launched the Global South Women in STEM (GSW-STEM) Network, connecting female tech leaders from Brazil, South Africa, India, Ethiopia, Cameroon, and beyond to advance technological development and female leadership across the Global South.
