About

Where Data Meets

I am a Data Scientist who combines rigorous modeling, quantitative methods, and deep contextual understanding of policy and human rights. After 7 years in applied research, I have built machine learning models, geospatial analyses, and evidence-based reports for governments, NGOs, and global research firms across Brazil, Latin America, and the UK.

Currently studying at the London School of Economics (MPA, Data Science for Public Policy) while working as a Data Scientist at TwentySix Consulting in London.

Machine Learning & Human Rights

Deployed logistic regression, decision tree, and ensemble models using scikit-learn and statsmodels; applied QGIS for spatial feature engineering.

Data Advocacy

D3S Consultoria

D3S Consultoria is a Brazilian nonprofit I founded that provides free data analytics consulting, training, and technical products to NGOs that lack data expertise. Since 2024 we have impacted 25 organisations and indirectly reached over 35,000 people in vulnerable situations.

The organisation also runs a volunteer program to increase women's presence in STEM. In early 2026 we launched the Global South Women in STEM (GSW-STEM) Network, connecting female tech leaders from Brazil, South Africa, India, Ethiopia, Cameroon, and beyond.
